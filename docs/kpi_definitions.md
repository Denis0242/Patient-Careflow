# KPI Definitions

- **D1/D7/D30 Retention:** Share of patients retained after 1, 7, and 30 days.
- **Completion Rate:** Share of patients who completed treatment.
- **Outcome Success Rate:** Share of patients with successful outcomes.
- **Avg Cost per Patient:** Average treatment cost across patients.
- **Stage Conversion:** Share of patients moving from one funnel stage to the next.
